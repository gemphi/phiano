# 184 · Composition, and what a program DOES

In Phient's world, a word (w) is a seed: what it relates to, under what conditions (c), grows from it. The Phical paradigm, X ⇒ Y, drives and composes Phient's Phasor Language Models: Phinet, Phiano, PhiTon. Phical stores no point: a word is 32–64 relations, each a complex phasor on a closed circle with a count; meaning is a side effect of pairs of words moving under a condition.

## Step 1 — richer molds met their condition, and exposed it

Two levers from docs/183: bigger hole pools (8 names, 9 figures) and COMPOSITION — one mold draw in seven joins a second proven building on the end, binding classes re-based (MoldSet::write gained a compose rate; buildings composed, not leaves). Five-seed means, 10,000 rounds: volume 6,145 (5,977–6,425) against the mold-only 5,355, rate held at 64.6%, and new skeletons exploded to 884 (389–1,652). The fixed condition — volume accelerating past the old line at unchanged purity — MET.

And the new column installed beside it told the truth: DISTINCT BEHAVIOURS (what was written + what was returned) were 116 for the scribe against 112 for the filler — equal. The structural explosion does nothing. Mechanically: concatenated buildings share the one start slot, the machine enters the LAST one, and the first building is dead weight. Composition as concatenation is structurally novel and behaviourally dead. String novelty had become farmable (the collapse of docs/180, one level up), so it can no longer be the currency.

## Step 2 — the interest rule moved to behaviour

One line again: a success is NOVEL only if it BEHAVES newly — writes or returns something no kept program has. Five-seed means under the behavioural gate:

| arm | ran | behaviours (range) | new skeletons |
|---|---|---|---|
| soup | 0.0% | 0 | 0 |
| token | 4.9% | 5 (2–15) | 4 |
| mold | 71.0% | **175 (139–229)** | 6 |
| scribe | 62.2% | 134 (125–149) | 33 |

Reported straight: under the measure that matters, the PLAIN FILLER now beats the coupled scribe. Wandering and dead composition are taxes once behaviour is the currency — at THIS semantic envelope, where two seed programs' arithmetic is the entire space of things a program can do, and filling figures explores it fastest.

## What the round settles

The currency ladder is now explicit and each rung was farmed then fenced: rate (farmed by one trivial program, docs/180) → distinct strings (farmed by substitution at scale, docs/181–183) → distinct structures (farmed by concatenation, this round) → **distinct behaviours**, which nothing yet farms. The ceiling on behaviour is the seeds' semantics: new behaviour needs new semantic material — compositions whose buildings actually CALL each other (one start, linked names), richer shown programs, and the quest writing uses of what the scribe is unsure of. Condition for the next rung, fixed now: behaviours accelerate past the 175 line, same protocol, five seeds.
