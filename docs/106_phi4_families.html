Phi-4's Families

A word is nothing but what it relates to, under what conditions.

Twelve categories from Phi-4's own answers in the night quest: 3,630 relations about 209 words. In none of the twelve does one relation hold for every member. The members hang together the way fibres make a thread: many short overlaps, none running the whole length.

The thread: relations held by two or more members

Each row is a relation Phi-4 gave; each dot is a member that holds it. No row is full. Select a member to follow its column.

Who shares with whom

A line joins two members that share at least one relation; thicker lines share more.

Words in more than one family

Phi-4 filed these words under several categories, so a fibre can pass from one thread into another.

Source: Phiano docs/105 and paper 21 (Descriptive Logic III). A member's relations are its has, part-of, made-of, used-for and does answers; is-a is left out because it defines the category. Categories need at least five members with at least three relations each. Random sets are 200 groups of the same size drawn from all 209 words.

Limit: the quest asked each word only a few questions per kind, so a relation Phi-4 knows but was never asked about does not appear here.
