# 106 · The rough ground, a surveyable view, and methods

In Phient's world, a word (w) is nothing but what it relates to, under what conditions (c). The Phical paradigm, X ⇒ Y, drives and composes Phient's Phasor Language Models: Phinet, Phiano, PhiTon. Phical stores no point: a word is 32–64 relations, each a complex phasor on a closed circle with a count; meaning is a side effect of pairs of words moving under a condition.

The write-up is paper 22, `docs/papers/22_DESCRIPTIVE_LOGIC_4_ROUGH_GROUND.md` (and `.pdf`), which reads Philosophical Investigations §§89–133.

## Contradictions (§§107, 125)

`bin/family --rough`. Read by the kinds' ideal meaning, 210 of the night table's 3,630 rows (5.8%) contradict each other:

| contradiction | rows |
|---|---|
| a is-a b and b is-a a | 39 |
| a has b and a part-of b | 164 |
| a part-of b and b part-of a | 12 |

phinet is trained on the told rows with these contradicting rows and without them, then read on the same 702 held-out rows, none of which is itself a contradicting row. Over 4 seeds, held-out MRR is 0.079 ± 0.006 with them and 0.082 ± 0.009 without. The difference is within the seed spread. Raw table: `106_rough_ground.md`.
