# 185 · Closing the scribe line — status

In Phient's world, a word (w) is a seed: what it relates to, under what conditions (c), grows from it. The Phical paradigm, X ⇒ Y, drives and composes Phient's Phasor Language Models: Phinet, Phiano, PhiTon. Phical stores no point: a word is 32–64 relations, each a complex phasor on a closed circle with a count; meaning is a side effect of pairs of words moving under a condition.

## Linked composition: built, measured, NOT MET

MoldSet now keeps a library of callee fragments (each top-level LET that is not start, split at section depth 0, with the binding class of its declared name) and a composition PREPENDS a callee and ties one of the caller's classes to the callee's name — a building the program can call, not dead weight. Five seeds, 10,000 rounds, behavioural gate: mold 71.4% / 180 behaviours (139–229); scribe 61.9% / 135 (125–145). The line to beat was 175; linking did not move behaviour. The filler remains the best writer at this semantic envelope, and the ceiling is the seeds' semantics, not the composition mechanism.
