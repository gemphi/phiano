# Phient papers — sixteen papers, January to September 2026

In Phient's world, a word (w) is nothing but what it relates to, under what conditions (c). The Phical paradigm, X ⇒ Y, drives and composes Phient's Phasor Language Models: Phinet, Phiano, PhiTon. Phical stores no point: a word is 32–64 relations, each a complex phasor on a closed circle with a count; meaning is a side effect of pairs of words moving under a condition.

Phient's own series: what the Phasor Language Models are, what has been built, what has been measured and what has not, written from the source code and the numbered docs. Each paper names the actual types, functions and commands, cites every number exactly from the docs or `PHIENT_FACTS.md`, says plainly what is not built and not measured, and ends with the local paths it read. The PML family throughout: phinet the parent; phical, phinum, phiqlm (planned), phifno and phiton its children; phiano the word application, phiecs the world and the quest, phiace the UI.
