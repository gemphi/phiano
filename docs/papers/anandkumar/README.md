# Anima Anandkumar, read in Phient's terms — sixteen papers, January to September 2026

In Phient's world, a word (w) is nothing but what it relates to, under what conditions (c). The Phical paradigm, X ⇒ Y, drives and composes Phient's Phasor Language Models: Phinet, Phiano, PhiTon. Phical stores no point: a word is 32–64 relations, each a complex phasor on a closed circle with a count; meaning is a side effect of pairs of words moving under a condition.

Sixteen papers by Anima Anandkumar's group, from the tensor methods of 2012 to FourCastNet 3 of 2025, each read as a paper of our own: what it claims (fetched from the source, nothing invented), the mathematics plainly, its experiments as reported, and then the interpretation in Phical terms with the experiment that would test it inside phinet. Every Phient number in every paper comes from `PHIENT_FACTS.md` and is cited exactly; every fact about her from `../17_ANIMA_ANANDKUMAR_PROFILE.md`. Sixteen sections each, about seven thousand words apiece, dated fortnightly from 12 January to 14 September 2026 in the order her work happened. The brief the writers followed is `BRIEF.md`.
