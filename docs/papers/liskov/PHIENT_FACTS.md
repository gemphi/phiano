# Phient facts an interpreter may cite (all measured, all in gemphi)

Paradigm (open every paper with this, verbatim):
"In Phient's world, a word (w) is nothing but what it relates to, under what conditions (c). The Phical paradigm, X ⇒ Y, drives and composes Phient's Phasor Language Models: Phinet, Phiano, PhiTon. Phical stores no point: a word is 32–64 relations, each a complex phasor on a closed circle with a count; meaning is a side effect of pairs of words moving under a condition."

Family (PML = Phi machine learning): phinet is the parent engine (Tensor [b,w,s] complex, Tape autograd, unitary FFT, Fno, joint training, HRR, 64 hexagram gates, sphere). Children: phifno 1.0.0 (spherical operator), phical (geometry of the relation circle), phiton (light as units: LightQuantum, SpectralBand, PhitonColor), phinum, phiqlm (planned, nothing built). phiano = word application (Facet, Trainer, generation, personas, quest ingest, helix). phiecs = entity-component world + quest. PhiTon = light units.
