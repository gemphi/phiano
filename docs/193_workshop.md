# 193 · The workshop — 129 units of the guide played as 65 pages

In Phient's world, a word (w) is a seed: what it relates to, under what conditions (c), grows from it. The Phical paradigm, X ⇒ Y, drives and composes Phient's Phasor Language Models: Phinet, Phiano, PhiTon. Phical stores no point: a word is 32–64 relations, each a complex phasor on a closed circle with a count; meaning is a side effect of pairs of words moving under a condition.

The Key holds 3872 answers across 143 units; it is never told — after each lesson the student completes the unit's answers word by word, and is graded.

Each page: what the student BROUGHT to the unit (next-word top-1 on its sentences before being told them), the unit told on the fly, the quest prompts it would send a teacher for the constructions it is unsure of, the unit's own exercises as workshop prompts, and one task of logic handed to the compiler and judged. Generated by `examples/workshop.rs`; nothing here is written by hand.
