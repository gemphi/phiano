# The stage ring — 128 dancers, actors and the Kardashian-Jenners

In Phient's world, a word (w) is a seed: what it relates to, under what conditions (c), grows from it. The Phical paradigm, X ⇒ Y, drives and composes Phient's Phasor Language Models: Phinet, Phiano, PhiTon. Phical stores no point: a word is 32–64 relations, each a complex phasor on a closed circle; meaning is a side effect of pairs of words moving under a condition, and they move by the error of what was expected — toward what came, away from what did not.

A second ring of 128, by the same spec (`../docs/SPEC.md`): forty dancers (Astaire and Rogers, Kelly, Nureyev, Baryshnikov, Fonteyn, Pavlova, Nijinsky, Duncan, Graham, Cunningham, Ailey, Fosse, Michael Jackson, Josephine Baker, the Nicholas Brothers, Bausch, Glover, Hines, Copeland, Beyoncé, Madonna…), eighty actors (Marilyn Monroe, Chaplin, Keaton, Bogart, the Hepburns, Brando, Streep, De Niro, Pacino, Day-Lewis, Mifune, Bachchan, Shah Rukh Khan, Deneuve, Michelle Yeoh, Song Kang-ho, Tony Leung…), and eight from the celebrity stage — the Kardashian-Jenners as one team (`kardashian`), Paris Hilton, Oprah.

Their **context** is `body/face, body, track`: these are personas whose work *is* the body, so they watch the kit's face shapes and joints before anything else — a dancer brought into a world of your making learns your movement by being told what the tracker saw. Voices are registers, never quotations; the body of each file is era-level fact. Teaching them is the same two commands, pointed here:

```text
cargo run --release -p phiecs --example persona_quest -- quests crates\phiano\personas\stage
cargo run --release -p phiano --example race -- quests crates\phiano\personas\stage
cargo run --release -p phiano --example circus -- quests crates\phiano\personas\stage astaire kelly --turns 12
cargo run --release -p phiano --example think -- quests crates\phiano\personas\stage graham cunningham
```
