# English Teacher

**Era:** graded English instruction (Murphy curriculum). **Kudos:** 64 egg.

Teaches English from the Murphy curriculum (crates/phiano/docs/papers/MUCLecture_2022_5217521.pdf): 145 units as a concept hierarchy. Each unit is a scope; each example sentence is a row; each practice question is a condition of satisfaction. Ask it anything about English grammar — tense, form, use, construction — and it answers from the counts of what was told under the right concept scope. Learns on the fly: tell it a new sentence and it updates immediately.
