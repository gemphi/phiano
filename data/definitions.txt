# Royalty and governance
monarch: a sovereign head of state especially a king queen or emperor
king: a male sovereign ruler of an independent state
queen: a female sovereign ruler of an independent state
sovereign: possessing supreme or ultimate power and authority
ruler: a person who rules or governs a nation or people
kingdom: a country state or territory ruled by a king or queen
palace: the official residence of a sovereign or high-ranking leader
throne: the royal seat of a monarch used as a symbol of authority
crown: a circular ornamental headdress worn by a monarch as a symbol of authority
royal: relating to or befitting a king or queen
emperor: the supreme ruler of an empire
empire: an extensive group of states or countries ruled over by a single monarch
authority: the power or right to give orders and make decisions

# Animals
dolphin: a highly intelligent aquatic marine mammal known for social behavior
mammal: a warm-blooded vertebrate animal that nourishes young with milk
ocean: a very large expanse of saltwater that covers most of the earth
sea: the expanse of salt water that covers most of the earths surface
whale: a very large marine mammal that breathes air through a blowhole
fish: a limbless cold-blooded vertebrate animal with gills and fins
shark: a large marine predator fish with cartilaginous skeleton
water: a clear colorless liquid essential for life

# Nature
tree: a woody perennial plant with a single main trunk and branches
forest: a large area covered chiefly with trees and undergrowth
river: a large natural stream of water flowing in a channel to the sea
mountain: a large natural elevation of the earths surface rising abruptly
sky: the region of the atmosphere and outer space seen from the earth
sun: the star around which the earth orbits providing light and heat
moon: the natural satellite of the earth visible by reflected sunlight
star: a massive luminous sphere of plasma held together by gravity
light: the natural agent that stimulates sight and makes things visible
dark: having little or no light

# Human and society
human: a member of the species homo sapiens characterized by intelligence
person: a human being regarded as an individual
society: the aggregate of people living together in a more or less ordered community
family: a group of related individuals living together or sharing common ancestry
friend: a person with whom one has a bond of mutual affection
love: an intense feeling of deep affection
hate: a feeling of intense or passionate dislike
happy: feeling or showing pleasure or contentment
sad: feeling or showing sorrow or unhappiness

# Knowledge and communication
word: a single distinct meaningful element of speech or writing
language: a system of communication used by a particular community or country
book: a written or printed work consisting of pages bound in covers
read: to look at and comprehend the meaning of written or printed matter
write: to mark letters words or symbols on a surface with a pen or pencil
learn: to gain or acquire knowledge of or skill in something by study or experience
teach: to impart knowledge to or instruct someone in how to do something
know: to be aware of through observation inquiry or information
think: to use the mind actively to form ideas or opinions
question: a sentence worded to elicit information
answer: a thing said written or done as a reaction to a question
mind: the element of a person that enables awareness and thought

# Science and technology
science: the intellectual and practical activity encompassing systematic study
energy: the capacity or power to do work such as to move an object
force: strength or energy as an attribute of physical action or movement
wave: a disturbance that transfers energy through matter or space
phase: a distinct stage in a process of change or development
frequency: the rate at which something occurs or is repeated over time
amplitude: the maximum extent of a vibration or oscillation measured from equilibrium
resonance: the reinforcement or prolongation of sound by reflection or by synchronous vibration
spectrum: a band of colors produced by separation of light by wavelength
color: the property possessed by an object of producing different sensations on the eye

# Engineering and building
engine: a machine with moving parts that converts power into motion
machine: an apparatus using or applying mechanical power and having several parts
build: to construct something by putting parts or materials together
design: a plan or drawing produced to show the look and function of something
project: an individual or collaborative enterprise that is carefully planned
road: a wide way leading from one place to another
bridge: a structure built to span a physical obstacle such as a body of water

# Time and space
time: the indefinite continued progress of existence and events in the past present and future
space: a continuous area or expanse that is free available or unoccupied
world: the earth together with all of its countries and peoples
life: the condition that distinguishes active organisms from inorganic matter
death: the end of the life of a person or organism
begin: to perform or undergo the first part of an action or activity
end: a final part of something especially a period of time or story
