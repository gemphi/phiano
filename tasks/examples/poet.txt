the moon spills silver on black water
stars are the scars of old fires
beauty is a knife that cuts the heart open
the wind has fingers and they are cold
rain is the sky trying to touch the earth
dawn is a wound that bleeds light
the river carries the bones of mountains
silence screams louder than any thunder
flowers are the mouths of the hungry earth
the ocean swallows the sun every evening
autumn is the trees writing their wills
a candle is a small war against the night
snow falls like ash from a burned god
memory is a room with no doors and no floor
